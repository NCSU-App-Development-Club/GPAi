## Delete Your GPAi Account

To delete your GPAi account and all associated data, open the GPAi app and follow these steps:

1. Tap the profile icon in the top navigation bar.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This will immediately delete:

- Your server-side session
- Any content reports you have filed

Your local data (transcript and chat history) is stored on your device and will be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Rate-limit data associated with your account expires automatically within 24 hours.

If you are unable to access the app, you may also request account deletion by emailing [ncsuappdevelopmentclub@gmail.com](mailto:ncsuappdevelopmentclub@gmail.com) with the subject line "GPAi Account Deletion" from the email address associated with your account.
